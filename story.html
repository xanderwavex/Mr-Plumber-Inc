How We Got Here

It started in 1958 with one man, one truck and a name on the door: Boyd Plumbing. Four generations of the same family have carried the trade since, widening the work, adding crews, and taking on the Midlands' businesses alongside its homes.

Our Story

Same Family. Same Standard.

In 2002–2003 the company was incorporated and took the name it carries today, Mr. Plumber, Inc. The work grew, the crews grew, and the commercial accounts came on, but the way we treat a customer's home never changed.

Today the fourth generation runs the trucks, the crews and the emergency line across Columbia and the Midlands. Still family owned. Still answering the phone.
