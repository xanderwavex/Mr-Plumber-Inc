A Few Common Questions

Which Jobs Can I Finance?

The bigger ones: water heaters, tankless systems, whole-house repiping, sewer line work, bathroom remodels and water treatment.

Can I Upgrade To Tankless With Financing?

Yes. Tankless systems are covered, so you can make the switch without paying the full cost up front.

How Do I Get Started?

Mention financing when you request a quote, or ask your technician on site. We'll walk you through the options.

Will I Know My Monthly Payment Up Front?

Yes. You'll see the total and the monthly payment before any work begins, and decide from there.

What About Smaller Repairs?

Financing is set up for larger jobs. For everyday repairs, we accept cash, check and credit card.

Do I Have To Use Financing?

No. It's just one option. You can always pay by cash, check or credit card instead.
