Why Homeowners Switch

The Case For Going Tankless

  • Endless Hot WaterHot water that doesn't run out.

    Showers, dishwasher and laundry at once, and nobody gets a cold surprise. A correctly sized unit heats water as it flows, so there's no tank to empty.

  • Lower Energy BillsNo paying to keep a tank hot.

    A tank heater keeps gallons of water hot whether you use them or not. A tankless unit only fires when you open a tap, so there's no standby loss.

  • Lasts About Twice As LongAround 20 years of service life.

    Roughly 20 years of service life versus 10–12 for a typical tank, with annual descaling to keep it running at its best.

  • Reclaims Your SpaceWall-mounted, no bulky tank.

    A wall-mounted unit frees up the footprint of a bulky tank in the closet, garage or utility room.

  • No Tank To FloodNothing waiting to rupture.

    There's no 50-gallon tank sitting there waiting to rupture and soak the floor around it.

  • Cleaner Hot WaterHeated fresh, not stored.

    Water isn't sitting for days collecting sediment and rust. It's heated fresh as it passes through the unit.

Your Options

The Tankless Units We Install

Which one fits depends on your home's fuel, your hot-water demand and where the unit will live. We size it with you.

Natural Gas

The usual choice for whole-house use. Highest flow rates. Needs proper gas supply and venting.

Propane

Full tankless performance for homes without a natural gas line. We size the gas supply and venting to match.

Condensing

A high-efficiency gas unit that reclaims heat from its own exhaust to lower operating cost.

Whole-House

One correctly sized unit serving the entire home, the typical tank replacement.

Point-of-Use

A small dedicated unit so you're not waiting for hot water to travel to a distant tap.

With A Recirculation Pump

Near-instant hot water at the tap and less wasted water. Good for larger homes.
